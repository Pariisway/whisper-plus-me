Night Owls: someone's awake at 3am too

When the house is quiet and your mind isn't, Night Owls is where people gather. Insomniacs, night-shift workers, new parents on feeding duty and overthinkers keep each other company until the sun comes up. Talk, listen, or just leave the room playing in the background.

🌙 Join Night Owls free👀 Just listen first

Free · No download · 18+ · Open 24/7

What happens in Night Owls

  • People awake at the same odd hours as you
  • Calm, low-key conversation
  • A live room to listen to while you wind down
  • Members around the world, so someone's always on

Good for: Insomnia, night shifts, jet lag, or the 3am thoughts that won't stop.

How to join

  1. Tap Join Night Owls. You can peek in as a guest first.
  2. Sign up free with email or Google, and pick any display name.
  3. Type in the chat, or tap Live to talk out loud. Click someone's name to call them 1-on-1.

More on Whisper+Me

Besides live rooms, you can call members 1-on-1 (creators set their own price), host your own paid room, and meet people in real life at M&G meet & greets and Connect Me date-night packages at real, public places.

Questions

Is the Night Owls room free?

Yes. Joining Whisper+Me, reading and writing in the chat, and listening to the live mic are free. Members can go live on the mic for free too. Coins are only needed for 1-on-1 calls and paid member rooms.

Do I have to use my real name?

No. Pick any display name you like. You can add a photo if you want, but you don't have to.

Do I need to download an app?

No. Whisper+Me runs in your browser on any phone or computer. You can add it to your home screen in one tap so it opens like an app and rings for calls.

Who can join?

Adults 18 and over. Rooms are moderated, and you can block or report anyone with one tap.
