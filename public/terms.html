  • To operate the call and coin system.

Location is only collected when you explicitly enable it in the app. You can turn it off at any time from your profile settings. Location data is not shared with third parties beyond what is necessary to display the live map feature.

Account data is retained until you request deletion. Call records are retained for up to 90 days for fraud prevention. Chat messages may be retained indefinitely as part of the community log.
