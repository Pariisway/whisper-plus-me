6. M&G (Meet & Greet) and Connect Me

M&G lets members meet in person at public places. Each M&G is hosted either by a member ("host") or by Whisper+Me, and is sold one spot per person. M&G is a social, in-person, public meet-up feature. It is not a dating, escort, companionship-for-pay or adult service, and none of those may be offered, requested or arranged through it.

  • Age: you must be at least 21 to buy a spot or host an M&G. We ask your birth year the first time.
  • Public places only: every M&G must take place at a real, public business or venue with a working address and phone number. Private homes, hotel rooms and similar locations are not allowed.
  • Group orders: you can buy up to 6 spots in one order for you and friends. One code covers your group, and refunds (when they apply) cover the whole order.
  • Address after purchase: the exact address and the place's phone number are shared privately with you after you pay, with your entry code. Do not share them publicly.
  • Spots are non-refundable, except when the host or Whisper+Me cancels, the host doesn't show up, or the M&G doesn't happen as described. In those cases you get your full amount back as Whisper+Me credit right away, which you can use on any M&G or Connect Me. If you'd rather have it on your card, tap Send it to my card and it is refunded to the card you paid with (usually within 5–10 days). Credit has no cash value otherwise and can't be transferred.
  • Reporting a problem: if something goes wrong, use Report a problem on your ticket before the host is paid (about 3 days after the M&G). Reports pause the host's payment while we look into it.

Hosting an M&G

  • Review before publishing: every member-hosted M&G is reviewed by Whisper+Me before it goes on sale. We may approve it, ask you to change it, or reject it. Changes to the time, place or price after it's live are reviewed again.
  • Contact details: hosts must give a working email and phone number. Whisper+Me may contact you, and may call the venue, to verify a booking. You can edit these details any time under ⭐ M&Gs you host.
  • Host deposit: to host you keep a deposit on your account equal to the price of one spot of your M&G (or $5 if you are selling only one spot). You pay it once; it stays on your account and covers all your future M&Gs, and you only pay more if a later M&G needs a bigger deposit. It is not returned after each M&G. The part covering an M&G is kept if you cancel within 48 hours of the start after spots are sold, or don't show up, to help cover refund costs, and you receive a strike. If you stop hosting and have no M&Gs in progress, you can ask support to refund the rest of your deposit. Repeated strikes may pause your access to M&G.
  • Fees and payment: Whisper+Me keeps a platform fee (currently 10%) of each spot sold. Card processing fees are paid by Whisper+Me out of its share, not by hosts. The rest is added to your Whisper+Me earnings about 3 days after your M&G, once you've checked in your guests and no buyer has reported a problem, and is paid out through Stripe Connect like other earnings.
  • Attending: hosts must attend in person and check in guests using their codes. If no guests are checked in, or a buyer reports a problem, Whisper+Me reviews the M&G before paying the host and may refund buyers instead.
  • You are responsible for any venue costs, reservations, and the conduct of your M&G. Whisper+Me is not a party to arrangements between hosts, guests and venues.

Connect Me

  • Connect Me packages are night-out packages at a real venue for a set date and time, sold by Whisper+Me for a group (for example, dinner for 2). The buyer pays once and invites the people they bring, on Whisper+Me or with a link.
  • Everyone attending must have a free Whisper+Me account and be at least 21. Each person gets their own code; the address is shared after purchase or acceptance.
  • Packages are non-refundable once bought, including if someone you invited doesn't come. If the venue or Whisper+Me cancels, the buyer gets the full amount back as Whisper+Me credit, which can be sent to the original card on request.
  • Check-in: each person taps "I'm here" at the venue, which checks your phone's location against the venue, or shows their code to staff. If you're checked in and someone on your package doesn't show, we may add a small reward to your account.
  • Connect Me is a social night-out product, not a dating, escort or companionship-for-pay service.

21+ events with ID at the door

  • Some M&Gs and Connect Me packages are marked 🪪 21+ ID (for example, at a bar). To buy or accept one you confirm you'll bring a valid government photo ID. The host, the venue or Whisper+Me may check it, and you can be turned away if you can't show one or are under 21. In that case the spot is not refunded.
  • Hosts of 21+ M&Gs must check each guest's photo ID before checking them in. Venues may also check ID under their own rules and the law.

Ratings

  • After an M&G or Connect Me, people who had a spot can rate it from 1 to 5 stars for up to 14 days. Star averages appear on host profiles and on packages. Written comments are only seen by Whisper+Me. We may remove ratings that are abusive, fake or unrelated.

Meet only in public, tell a friend where you'll be, and report anything that feels wrong to support. Whisper+Me does not run background checks on members.
