Whisper+Me: free voice chat rooms, 1-on-1 calls and real meet-ups

Whisper+Me is a live social app that runs in your browser. Drop into a voice chat room and talk (or just listen), take the open mic, call creators 1-on-1, and meet people in real life at public places. It's free to join and open 24/7. 18+.

Voice chat rooms

  • General: talk to new people, any time
  • Vent & Release: get it off your chest, anonymously
  • Anxiety & Stress and Mental Health: peer support from people who get it
  • Night Owls: for anyone awake at 3am
  • Grief & Loss: talk about the person you miss
  • Live Voices: an online open mic
  • LGBTQ+ Safe Space, Astrology, Tarot and more rooms

1-on-1 calls

Call members and creators privately. Creators set their own price and earn $2 for every coin spent on their calls and rooms.

Meet up in real life

M&G meet & greets let you meet hosts and new people at real, public venues. Connect Me has ready-made date-night packages (dinner, bowling, shows and more) at one price for 2 or 4. No date ideas? Start here.
