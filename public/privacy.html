Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Information We Collect

  • Account data: email address, display name, profile photo, bio, social links.
  • Your public profile: your display name, photo, bio, links, call price and number of calls make up your profile page (whisperplusme.com/u/…). Anyone with the link can see it, and search engines like Google can show it once it has a photo or a bio. Remove both to keep it out of search; your email is never shown.
  • Usage data: call history (caller/receiver IDs, timestamps, coin amounts).
  • Location data: latitude/longitude when you enable location sharing. Deleted immediately when you turn it off.
  • Payment data: handled entirely by Stripe. We do not store card numbers. We keep a record of your purchases, any Whisper+Me credit, and any host deposit on file.
  • Notification data: if you turn on alerts, a push token for each device so we can ring it for incoming calls and send event updates. Tokens for devices that stop working are deleted automatically.
  • Check-in location: when you tap "I'm here" for a Connect Me package, your location at that moment is compared with the venue's and only the result (and time) is kept.
  • Safety data: people you block, and reports you send about other members.
  • Ratings: the stars and any comment you give an M&G or Connect Me. Your stars count toward public averages; your comment is only seen by Whisper+Me.
  • Email: we use your account email to send receipts, your entry codes, reminders the day before, payout notices and credit notices. These are about things you did on the site; we don't sell or share your email.
  • Home Screen app: if you add Whisper+Me to your Home Screen, we note that you did so we know how many members use it.
  • Chat messages: community chat messages are stored in Firebase and visible to all logged-in users.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To operate the call and coin system.
  • To display your profile to other members.
  • To process payouts via Stripe Connect.
  • To detect and prevent fraud and abuse.

3. Location Data

Location is only collected when you explicitly enable it in the app. You can turn it off at any time from your profile settings. Location data is not shared with third parties beyond what is necessary to display the live map feature.

4. Data Sharing

  • Agora: voice call data passes through Agora's RTC servers. See Agora's Privacy Policy.
  • Stripe: payment and payout data. See Stripe's Privacy Policy.
  • Firebase (Google): database, authentication, and storage. See Firebase Privacy.
  • Google reCAPTCHA: checks that visits come from real people, not bots. Its use is subject to the Google Privacy Policy and Terms.
  • We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

5. Cookies, Analytics & Advertising

  • Analytics: we use Google Analytics to understand how the site is used (pages visited, device type, rough location by country or city). It uses cookies.
  • Advertising: some pages show ads from Google AdSense. Google and its partners use cookies to show ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You can turn off personalized ads at Google Ads Settings, or opt out of many third-party vendors at aboutads.info. Learn more about how Google uses information from sites that use its services.
  • Meta (Facebook): we use Facebook's tools to measure visits from our Facebook and Instagram ads.
  • Ad tracking links: when you arrive from one of our ads or shared links, we note which one so we know which ads work. This is stored with your account and purchases.
  • You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Some features may not work without them.

6. Data Retention

Account data is kept until you delete your account. Call history is kept so you can see your call log and so earnings, payouts and refunds add up; records needed for payments and taxes are kept as long as the law requires. Each chat room keeps only its newest messages; older ones are deleted automatically.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction you may have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data. Contact us at whisper.me.support@gmail.com to exercise these rights. You can also delete your account yourself from 👤 Profile → More → Delete my account.

8. Children

Whisper+Me is not intended for users under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from minors. If you believe a minor has created an account, contact us immediately.

9. Security

We use Firebase Security Rules, server-side token verification, and Stripe's PCI-compliant infrastructure to protect your data. No system is 100% secure; use the Service at your own risk.
