🔐 Keep emails private

Older accounts stored their email on their public profile, where any member could read it. This moves every email into private storage that only the member and you can see. Safe to run more than once.

💰 Money

What came in, what you kept, and who's owed.

–Collected from coin sales
–Your cut (from coins spent)
–Coins bought, not spent yet
–Owed to creators

“Collected” counts the moment someone buys coins. “Your cut” grows only when a coin is spent on a call or room ($1 per coin). The gap is coins still sitting in people's balances.

💸 Creator payouts

Everything creators and hosts earn (calls, rooms, M&Gs) adds up here. Card fees already came out of your share, so they get the full amount. 💸 Pay now sends it straight to their bank through Stripe (they need $50+ and payouts set up in Profile → Wallet). Payments people make take about 2 days to become available in your Stripe balance.

💡 Short loops work best: 10–30 seconds, MP4, 720p, under 15 MB each. GIFs are usually much bigger than the same clip as an MP4, so keep GIFs short (a few seconds, under 8 MB). Big files load slowly on phones and use more of your Firebase bandwidth. Visitors with “data saver” on see a still picture instead (add one picture to the playlist for them).
