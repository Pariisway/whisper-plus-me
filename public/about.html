Whisper+Me was built for one reason: everyone deserves to be heard. No face. No name. No judgment. Just real human voices, real connection, and real support — available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

💬

Why We Built Whisper+Me

The idea came from a simple, painful truth: millions of people go to bed every night with something heavy on their chest and no one safe to say it to. Text messages feel hollow. Video calls feel exposed. Therapy has a waiting list. Friends get tired.

Paris Wade, Whisper+Me's founder, experienced this firsthand. The solution wasn't another social network. It was something closer to the concept behind the film The Listener — a trained, caring stranger on the other end of a line, ready to hear you out without agenda, without judgment, without ever knowing your name.

Whisper+Me makes that real. Anyone can join. Anyone can listen. Anyone can be heard.

🎙️

How It Works

Whisper+Me is a live, anonymous voice platform. You join a room, speak when you're ready, and real people listen. There are no profiles that expose your identity, no video feeds, and no recordings. Just voice — the most human form of communication — protected by anonymity.

You can join a public support room to speak with a group, or connect one-on-one with another member. Every interaction happens through our secure, real-time audio infrastructure. When the call ends, it's gone. Nothing is stored. Nothing is tracked.

Who Whisper+Me Is For

Whisper+Me is for anyone who has ever typed "I'm fine" when they weren't. For anyone who has something heavy to say and no safe place to say it. For people going through grief, anxiety, loneliness, relationship pain, identity struggles, or just a bad week.

It's for the person awake at 3am with a racing mind. For the teenager who can't talk to their parents. For the adult who feels like a burden to their friends. For anyone who has ever wished there was just someone — anyone — on the other end of the line.

You don't need to be in crisis to use Whisper+Me. You just need to feel like talking.

🛡️

Your Safety Is Our Priority

Every room is moderated. Our community guidelines are enforced strictly and swiftly. Users can report and block anyone at any time with a single tap. Accounts that violate our safe space standards are removed immediately.

We do not collect more data than we need. We do not sell your data. We do not share your identity. Our infrastructure uses end-to-end encrypted voice channels and Firebase's industry-standard security. For full details, read our Privacy Policy.
