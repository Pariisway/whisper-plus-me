How you're paid

  • M&Gs: 20% of the site fee on spots the people you bring buy, and on M&Gs they host.
  • Date packages (Connect Me): 10% of the package price, paid once the date happens (both people check in). Every package you create counts, no matter who buys it. Other packages count when they're bought through your link within 30 days, or by someone you brought.
  • The buyer pays Whisper+Me, Whisper+Me pays the place, and Whisper+Me pays you. Nobody else pays anybody.
  • Coins (calls and rooms) and Plan a Date posts don't earn partner money.
  • Earnings are on hold for 14 days (for refunds and chargebacks), then added to your Whisper+Me earnings.
  • Earnings go to your bank with regular payouts once they reach $50. Set up payouts once in 👤 Profile → Wallet.

Partner rules

  • Be honest about what Whisper+Me is. No fake reviews, made-up earnings, or promises we don't make.
  • Say it's a partner link when you post it (for example #ad or #partner).
  • No spam: no mass DMs, comment spam, or posting in groups that don't allow it.
  • No fake or duplicate accounts, and you can't earn from your own account.
  • Don't run paid ads on the name “Whisper+Me” or pretend to be Whisper+Me.
  • Matchmaking: only suggest members who switched on “Open to matchmaker intros” themselves, and only for a date at the package's public place. Never promise anyone money or anything beyond the date, and never ask anyone for money. Everyone decides for themselves whether to go.
  • If the rules are broken, we can pause your link and cancel earnings that are still on hold. We can change the program with notice on this page.
